# 实用英语核心语法 (12 个关键点)

> 目标：掌握这 12 个语法点，能听懂、能说出 80% 日常交流内容。
> 学法：**学一个点，立刻造 5 个自己的句子说出来**，不刷题不背书。

---

## 第 1 点：Be 动词（最最基础）

**三种形式：** am / is / are

**公式：** 主语 + be + 表语

| 主语 | be 动词 | 例句 |
|------|--------|------|
| I | am | I am happy. |
| He / She / It | is | She is a teacher. |
| You / We / They | are | They are friends. |

**否定：** 主语 + be + not
- I am not tired.
- He is not here.

**疑问：** Be + 主语 + 表语？
- Are you ready? → Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
- Is she a student? → Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.

**缩写（口语必备）：**
- I am → I'm
- He is → He's
- You are → You're
- They are → They're
- is not → isn't
- are not → aren't

**听说训练：**
- 听："How are you?" → 答："I'm fine, thanks."
- 听："Where are you from?" → 答："I'm from China."
- 听："What time is it?" → 答："It's 3 o'clock."

## 第 2 点：一般现在时（日常状态和习惯）

**用法：** 描述经常做的事、事实、状态

**公式：** 主语 + 动词原形（第三人称单数加 s/es）

| 主语 | 动词形式 | 例句 |
|------|---------|------|
| I / You / We / They | 原形 | I work in a company. |
| He / She / It | 原形 + s/es | She works in a company. |

**第三人称单数变化规则：**
- 一般加 s：work → works
- 以 s / x / ch / sh 结尾加 es：watch → watches
- 辅音 + y 变 ies：study → studies
- have 变 has

**否定：** 主语 + don't / doesn't + 动词原形
- I don't like coffee.
- He doesn't like coffee.

**疑问：** Do / Does + 主语 + 动词原形？
- Do you speak English? → Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
- Does she like music? → Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.

**听说训练：**
- "What do you do for a living?" → "I work as a designer."
- "Do you like pizza?" → "Yes, I do."
- "She always drinks coffee in the morning."（听写理解）

---

## 第 3 点：现在进行时（正在发生的事）

**用法：** 描述**此刻**正在做的事

**公式：** 主语 + be + 动词-ing

- I am working right now.
- She is reading a book.
- They are playing football.

**ing 形式规则：**
- 一般加 ing：work → working
- 去 e 加 ing：make → making
- 重读闭音节双写最后字母加 ing：run → running

**否定：** 主语 + be + not + 动词-ing
- I am not watching TV.

**疑问：** Be + 主语 + 动词-ing？
- Are you listening to me?
- What are you doing?

**听说训练：**
- "What are you doing right now?" → "I'm studying English."
- "Where is she going?" → "She's going to the store."
- 听到 "I'm having lunch with my family" 能秒懂

---

## 第 4 点：一般过去时（过去发生的事）

**用法：** 描述**过去**做过的事

**公式：** 主语 + 动词过去式

**规则变化：**
- 一般加 ed：work → worked
- 以 e 结尾加 d：like → liked
- 辅音 + y 变 ied：study → studied
- 重读闭音节双写最后字母加 ed：stop → stopped

**不规则变化（必背 30 个）：**
```
go → went | come → came | see → saw | take → took | give → gave
get → got | make → made | do → did | have → had | say → said
tell → told | think → thought | know → knew | feel → felt | find → found
leave → left | bring → brought | buy → bought | meet → met | eat → ate
drink → drank | read → read（发音不同）| write → wrote | speak → spoke
run → ran | sit → sat | stand → stood | win → won | lose → lost
```

**否定：** 主语 + didn't + 动词原形
- I didn't go to school yesterday.

**疑问：** Did + 主语 + 动词原形？
- Did you see the movie? → Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

**听说训练：**
- "What did you do last weekend?" → "I went hiking with my friends."
- "Where did you go on vacation?" → "I went to Japan."
- 听到 "I had a great time last night" 能秒懂

---

## 第 5 点：现在完成时（过去发生并影响现在）

**用法：** 描述**过去发生、但和现在有关**的事（经验、结果、持续）

**公式：** 主语 + have / has + 过去分词

- I have lived here for 5 years.（我住这 5 年了，还住着）
- She has finished her homework.（她做完了，**现在不做了**）
- Have you ever been to Paris?（你**这辈子**去过吗？）

**常见时间标志词：** ever（曾经）、never（从不）、already（已经）、yet（还）、just（刚刚）、recently（最近）、for（持续多久）、since（自从）

**不规则过去分词（必背）：**
```
go → gone | come → come | see → seen | take → taken | give → given
get → got → got | make → made | do → done | have → had | say → said
tell → told | think → thought | know → known | feel → felt | find → found
eat → eaten | drink → drunk | read → read | write → written | speak → spoken
be → been
```

**否定：** 主语 + haven't / hasn't + 过去分词

**疑问：** Have / Has + 主语 + 过去分词？

**与一般过去时的区别：**
- I lived in Beijing for 5 years.（我以前在北京住 5 年，**现在不住**了）
- I have lived in Beijing for 5 years.（我住这 5 年，**现在还住**）

**听说训练：**
- "Have you ever tried sushi?" → "Yes, I have. / No, I haven't."
- "I've been to London twice."
- "I haven't seen that movie yet."
- 听到 "I have just finished my work" 能秒懂

---

## 第 6 点：将来时（将来发生的事）

**用法：** 描述**将来**要做的事

### 方式 1：will + 动词原形（最常用）

- I will call you tomorrow.
- It will rain tomorrow.
- Will you come to the party? → Yes, I will.

### 方式 2：be going to + 动词原形（计划、打算）

- I am going to study abroad next year.
- What are you going to do this weekend?

**区别：**
- will：临时决定、预测
- be going to：事先计划、打算

**听说训练：**
- "What will you do this weekend?" → "I'll probably stay home."
- "I'm going to visit my parents next month."
- 听到 "We'll meet at 7 pm" 能秒懂

---

## 第 7 点：情态动词（表达能力和意愿）

**最常用的 6 个：**

### can / could：能力、请求、可能
- I can speak English.（我会说英语）
- Can you help me?（你能帮我吗？更礼貌：Could you help me?）
- It could rain later.（可能会下雨）

### will / would：意愿、请求
- I will help you.（我愿意帮你）
- Would you like some coffee?（你想要咖啡吗？超常用）
- Would you mind if I...?（你介意我...吗？）

### should / must：建议、必须
- You should see a doctor.（你应该看医生）
- I must go now.（我必须走了）

### may / might：可能、许可
- It may rain.（可能要下雨）
- May I use your phone?（我能用你电话吗？）

**听说训练：**
- "Could you speak more slowly, please?" → "Sure, no problem."
- "Would you like something to drink?" → "Yes, I'd like a coffee."
- "You should try this restaurant, it's great."
- 听到 "I can't find my keys" 能秒懂

## 第 8 点：比较级和最高级

**公式：**
- 比较级：A + be + 比较级 + than + B
- 最高级：A + be + the + 最高级 + of/in + 范围

**规则变化：**
- 一般加 er / est：tall → taller → tallest
- 以 e 结尾加 r / st：nice → nicer → nicest
- 辅音 + y 变 ier / iest：happy → happier → happiest
- 重读闭音节双写最后字母：big → bigger → biggest
- 双音节词前面加 more / most：beautiful → more beautiful → most beautiful

**不规则（必背）：**
```
good / well → better → best
bad → worse → worst
many / much → more → most
little → less → least
far → farther / further → farthest / furthest
```

**听说训练：**
- "This restaurant is better than that one."
- "She's the most talented person I know."
- "Today is worse than yesterday."

## 第 9 点：介词搭配（高频短语动词）

英语里很多动词和介词组合是固定搭配，必须整块记。

### 常用短语动词
```
look at 看 | look for 寻找 | look like 看起来像
look forward to 期待 | look up 查阅
listen to 听 | wait for 等待 | pay for 付钱
get up 起床 | get on 上车 | get off 下车 | get to 到达
get along with 与...相处 | get used to 习惯于
take off 起飞/脱下 | take out 拿出 | take care of 照顾
turn on 打开 | turn off 关闭 | turn down 调小/拒绝
turn into 变成 | turn up 出现/调大
put on 穿上 | put off 推迟 | put away 放好
pick up 捡起/接人 | pick out 挑出
give up 放弃 | give in 妥协 | give back 归还
find out 查明 | figure out 弄明白
run out of 用完 | look after 照顾 | look over 检查
```

**听说训练：**
- "I'm looking for a good restaurant. Do you have any recommendations?"
- "I'll pick you up at 6 pm."
- "Could you turn down the music a little?"
- 听到 "I can't figure out this problem" 能秒懂

## 第 10 点：连词和从句

**目的：** 把多个短句连成长句，让表达更自然。

### 时间连词
- when 当...时：I was reading when you called.
- while 当...时（持续）：While I was cooking, the phone rang.
- before / after / until / since

### 原因连词
- because 因为：I'm tired because I worked late.
- since 既然：Since you're here, let's start.

### 让步连词
- although / though 虽然：Although it rained, we had fun.
- even though 即使

### 条件连词
- if 如果：If it rains, I'll stay home.
- unless 除非：I'll go unless it rains.

### 宾语从句
- I think (that) he is right.
- She said (that) she was tired.
- Do you know what time it is?
- Can you tell me where the station is?

**听说训练：**
- "I'll call you when I arrive."
- "Although I'm busy, I'll make time for you."
- "If you need help, just let me know."
- 听到 "She said she would come later" 能秒懂

## 第 11 点：there be 句型

**用法：** 表达"某地有某物"

**公式：** There + be + 主语 + 地点/时间

- There is a book on the table.
- There are two cats in the room.
- There is going to be a meeting tomorrow.

**疑问：** Is / Are + there + 主语？
- Is there a bank near here? → Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.
- Are there any good restaurants? → Yes, there are.

**听说训练：**
- "Excuse me, is there a coffee shop nearby?"
- "There are 50 students in my class."
- 听到 "There used to be a park here" 能秒懂

## 第 12 点：常用句型（口语必背）

### 提建议 / 邀请
- Why don't you...? 你为什么不...？
- How about...? ...怎么样？
- Would you like to...? 你想...吗？
- Let's... 让我们...吧。
- Shall we...? 我们...好吗？

### 表达意见
- I think... / I don't think...
- In my opinion, ...
- As far as I'm concerned, ...
- It seems to me that...

### 请求许可
- Can I...? 我能...吗？
- May I...? 我可以...吗？
- Do you mind if I...? 你介意我...吗？
- Is it okay if I...? ...可以吗？

### 表示喜欢 / 不喜欢
- I love / like / enjoy + doing
- I'm fond of...
- I'm crazy about...（口语）
- I'm not a big fan of...（委婉）
- I can't stand...（受不了）
- I hate...

### 描述经历
- I have been to... 我去过...
- I have tried... 我试过...
- I have never... 我从未...
- I once... 我曾经...

### 表达频率
- always 总是 | usually 通常 | often 经常
- sometimes 有时 | rarely 很少 | never 从不
- every day 每天 | once a week 一周一次
- from time to time 偶尔

**听说训练：**
- "Would you like to join us for dinner?" → "Sure, I'd love to."
- "Can I ask you a question?" → "Of course, go ahead."
- "I'm not a big fan of spicy food."
- 听到 "Why don't we go for a walk?" 能秒懂

## 核心学习方法

### 1. 学语法不要"看书"——要"造句"
学一个时态，**立刻用这个时态说 5 句关于自己的话**：
- 学现在进行时：I am reading, I am working, I am learning...

### 2. 配听说训练
每个语法点配：
- 听：3 段使用该语法的音频
- 说：用这个语法造 5 个句子并录音

### 3. 错也要说
口语里语法错 10% 不影响交流，老外自己也会犯。**先流利，后准确**。

### 4. 跟读训练
- 找一段 1 分钟英语（Podcast、YouTube）
- 第 1 遍：听意思
- 第 2 遍：听每一句的语法结构
- 第 3 遍：暂停跟读
- 第 4 遍：影子跟读（与原音同时说）
- 第 5 遍：录音对比

### 5. 推荐教材（任选一本）
- **《赖世雄美语从头学》**：中文讲解，零基础友好
- **新概念英语第 1-2 册**：经典老牌，课文短小精悍
- **English Grammar in Use**：剑桥出的语法书，例句地道
- **《剑桥英语语法》**：中文版，分级清晰

### 6. 检验标准
- 看到句型能立刻反应中文意思
- 给中文 5 秒能说出英文
- 听一段对话能识别出用了哪个语法
- 录音自己的句子，老外能听懂
